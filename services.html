Weekly gatherings

Make room for what matters.

Gatherings are held at the church in Kitale. Contact us if you have a question before your first visit.

Sunday worship

Join us for praise, prayer, Bible teaching, and fellowship. Two Sunday worship services welcome the whole family.

02

Prayer meeting

Set aside time to pray together, encourage one another, and seek God's direction as a church family.

03
Weekly

Bible study

Grow in understanding through Scripture, conversation, questions, and shared learning.

Children's church

A safe, joyful space for children to learn the Bible, worship, and build friendships.

Youth fellowship
