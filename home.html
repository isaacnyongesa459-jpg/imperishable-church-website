A growing family, built on faith and fellowship.
Rooted in community

A little church with a wide-open heart.

Founded in 2020 by five committed believers, Imperishable Crown has grown into a welcoming spiritual family. Today, around 80–100 people gather each Sunday for worship, fellowship, and the teaching of God's Word.

Whether you are new to faith, returning to church, or looking for a place to belong, we would be glad to meet you.
